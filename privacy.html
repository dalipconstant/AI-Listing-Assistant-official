Privacy Policy

AI Listing Assistant (Chrome extension)

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

The extension processes product images and listing text on the page you are viewing (for example Meesho or Flipkart seller pages) to generate listing content. That data is sent to the AI provider you configure using your own API key (for example Google Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, or Grok).

Account and subscription data (if you sign in) may be stored with our auth/payment providers solely to manage your plan and access.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use a shared company API key to read your product images for our own training without your configuration.
  • We do not submit listings to marketplaces on your behalf.

API keys

API keys you enter are stored locally in the browser (Chrome extension storage) on your device so the extension can call the AI service you chose. Keep your keys private.

Third parties

When you generate listings, content is sent to the AI provider associated with your key under that provider’s terms. Payments (if any) are processed by our payment provider (for example Razorpay).
